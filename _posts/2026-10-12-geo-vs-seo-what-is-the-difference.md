GEO vs SEO comes down to the goal. SEO aims to rank your pages in a list of search results. GEO, or generative engine optimization, aims to get your brand named and cited inside the answers that AI tools write. [Farman Rind](https://farmanrind.com/) works on both, and this guide shows where they differ, where they overlap and what to do first.

## What is the difference between GEO and SEO?

The difference between GEO and SEO is where you want to appear. SEO targets a position in a results page that a person scans and clicks. GEO targets a mention or a citation inside an answer that an AI system composes.

| | SEO | GEO |
|---|---|---|
| Goal | Rank pages and earn clicks | Be cited or named in AI answers |
| Where you appear | A list of results | Inside a written answer |
| Unit of work | The page | The passage, the entity and the source |
| Main inputs | Relevance, links, page experience | Clear facts, named authors, trusted mentions |
| How you measure | Rankings, clicks, impressions | Mentions, citations, share of answers |

The research paper that coined the term, [GEO: Generative Engine Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735), frames GEO as a way to improve visibility in generative engine responses.

## Where do GEO and SEO overlap?

GEO and SEO overlap on the foundations. If a page cannot be crawled, indexed and understood, neither one works. Google's documentation on [AI features in Search](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) makes this clear: a page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet, and standard SEO practices still apply.

The shared basics are:

- Crawlable pages, with robots.txt that does not block what you want found.
- Internal links that connect related pages.
- Good page experience and fast loading.
- Important content kept in text, not hidden in images or scripts.
- Structured data that matches the visible page.

Google also says there are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. Strong SEO is the entry ticket.

## What does GEO add on top of SEO?

GEO adds work on passages, entities and third-party mentions. These matter because an AI system often builds an answer from short pieces of text taken from several sources.

1. **Answer-first passages.** Each section opens with a direct answer to one question.
2. **Entity clarity.** Your brand, people and services are named in full and described the same way across the web.
3. **Named authors.** Content carries a real byline and short bio.
4. **Freshness signals.** Pages show when they were last updated.
5. **Off-site mentions.** Other sites, communities and directories mention you, which gives AI systems more places to find you.
6. **Prompt testing.** You ask real buyer questions in AI tools and record who gets named.

## How do the metrics differ?

SEO metrics are mostly positions and clicks, while GEO metrics are mentions and citations. You can read SEO data in Google Search Console. GEO data usually comes from manual or tool-based testing of prompts.

For GEO, a simple record works: the question, the platform, the date, whether your brand was named, and which sources were cited. Repeat monthly. Google says traffic from its AI features is counted in the Search Console Performance report under the "Web" search type, so part of your GEO impact shows up in your existing SEO data.

## Which should you do first?

Do SEO foundations first, then add GEO. A site with indexing problems, thin pages or no clear service content should fix those before it chases AI citations.

1. Run an indexing and crawl check.
2. Fix the pages that matter most for revenue.
3. Rewrite key pages so each section answers one question first.
4. Add authors, dates and consistent business details.
5. Test 20 buyer questions in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google.
6. Repeat monthly and adjust.

If your SEO is already solid and competitors are named in AI answers while you are not, move GEO up your list.

## Does GEO replace SEO?

No. GEO sits on top of SEO. The same crawling and indexing work feeds both, and the same content quality helps both. Treat them as one program with two outputs: rankings and citations.

## FAQs

**Is GEO better than SEO?**

Neither is better, because they serve different goals. SEO earns rankings and clicks from search results. GEO earns mentions and citations inside AI answers. They rely on the same foundations, so improving crawlability, content quality and page experience helps both. Most businesses should run them together.

**Can I do GEO without SEO?**

Not effectively. AI products that search the web still need to crawl and index your pages. Google says a page must be indexed and eligible for a snippet to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. Without SEO basics, there is little for GEO work to build on.

**What is the difference between GEO and AEO?**

Both aim to get your content used in direct answers. AEO, answer engine optimization, is the older term and is often tied to featured snippets and voice answers. GEO usually refers to generative AI systems that write answers. In practice the work overlaps heavily.

**How do I measure GEO compared with SEO?**

SEO is measured with rankings, clicks and impressions in tools such as Search Console. GEO is measured by testing real questions in AI tools and recording whether your brand is named and which sources are cited. Repeat the same set monthly so results stay comparable.

**Do I need new content for GEO?**

Often you can start by improving existing pages. Rewrite sections so each one opens with a direct answer, add author names and dates, and fill gaps in questions buyers ask. New content helps where you have no page that answers a question buyers put to AI tools.
