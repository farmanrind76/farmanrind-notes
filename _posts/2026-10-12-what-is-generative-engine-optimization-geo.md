Generative engine optimization (GEO) is the practice of shaping your content so AI answer engines, such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, find it, understand it and cite it in their answers. This guide from [SEO and GEO consultant Farman Rind](https://farmanrind.com/) explains what GEO is, how it works and the first steps to take.

## What is generative engine optimization?

Generative engine optimization is the work of making a brand, page or person easy for AI systems to retrieve and quote. A generative engine writes an answer in its own words and names the sources it used. GEO aims to put your page among those sources.

The term comes from a 2023 research paper, [GEO: Generative Engine Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735), by Pranjal Aggarwal and five co-authors. The authors describe it as a framework for helping content creators improve their visibility in generative engine responses. They report gains in visibility of up to 40% in their tests. Treat that as a result from a research setting, not a promise for your site.

In plain terms, SEO asks "how do I rank this page?" and GEO asks "how do I get this page quoted in the answer?"

## How do AI engines choose what to cite?

AI engines choose sources by searching, retrieving pages and then picking passages that answer the question clearly. The exact rules differ by product, and the companies do not publish them in full. Two official documents give useful clues.

Google's guide to [AI features in Search](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) says a page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet. It also says there are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, and no special optimizations or markup are needed. It describes "query fan-out", where the system runs several related searches across subtopics to find supporting pages.

OpenAI's [bot documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) says OAI-SearchBot is the crawler used to surface websites in ChatGPT's search features. A site that blocks it will not appear in ChatGPT search answers.

The takeaway: be crawlable, be indexed, and answer sub-questions clearly.

## How is GEO different from SEO?

GEO differs from SEO in its goal and its unit of work. SEO targets a ranking position for a page. GEO targets a mention or citation inside a generated answer, and the system often pulls a short passage rather than a whole page.

- **Goal.** SEO wants clicks from a results list. GEO wants your name and link inside the answer.
- **Unit.** SEO optimizes pages. GEO optimizes passages, entities and sources.
- **Signals.** SEO leans on links and relevance. GEO also depends on clear facts, named authors and mentions across other sites.
- **Overlap.** Crawling, indexing, quality content and site speed matter to both.

GEO is not a replacement for SEO. A page that cannot be crawled or indexed has no chance of being cited.

## What does GEO work include?

GEO work covers your own site and the wider web. The list below is the core of what a practitioner does.

1. **Passages that stand alone.** Each section answers one question in the first sentence, so it still makes sense when quoted by itself.
2. **Clear entities.** Your brand, people and products are named in full and described the same way everywhere.
3. **Named authors.** Articles carry a real byline and a short bio.
4. **Fresh, dated content.** Pages show when they were last updated.
5. **Third-party mentions.** Other sites, directories, communities and publications mention you, because AI engines often cite sources outside the brand's own site.
6. **Crawler access.** Your robots.txt does not block the crawlers you want, such as OAI-SearchBot.
7. **Measurement.** You test the real questions your buyers ask and record whether you are named.

## How do you start with GEO?

Start with GEO by testing what AI tools say about you today. Guessing is how teams waste months, so gather a baseline first. Follow these steps.

1. Write 20 questions your buyers would ask an AI tool.
2. Ask each one in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google, and record whether your brand appears and which sources are cited.
3. Check that your key pages are indexed and not blocked in robots.txt.
4. Rewrite your top pages so each section opens with a direct answer.
5. Add author names, bios and last-updated dates.
6. Repeat the test every month and compare.

Nobody can promise that a given page will be cited, because the systems change and answers vary between runs. A monthly test shows you what is moving.

## Who needs GEO?

Any business whose buyers research with AI tools needs to watch GEO. That includes software companies, agencies, professional services and local firms. If competitors are named in AI answers and you are not, you have a visibility gap that traditional rank tracking will not show.

## FAQs

**Is generative engine optimization the same as SEO?**

No. SEO aims to rank pages in search results, while GEO aims to get your content cited inside AI-written answers. They share foundations such as crawling, indexing and quality content, so most businesses need both. A page that is not indexed in search has little chance of appearing in AI answers either.

**Does GEO work for small businesses?**

Yes, because AI tools answer local and niche questions too. A small business can win by being specific: clear service pages, a named owner, consistent business details and mentions on trusted local sites. You do not need a large budget to start. A monthly test of 20 questions costs time only.

**Do I need special markup for AI Overviews?**

Google says no. Its documentation states that no additional requirements or special optimizations are needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode. Standard SEO practice applies: be indexed, allow crawling, keep important content in text and make sure any structured data matches the visible page.

**How long does GEO take to show results?**

It varies by site, competition and how much needs fixing, and nobody can promise a date. Changes to crawler rules can take about a day to affect ChatGPT search, according to OpenAI. Content and mention work takes longer. Track your monthly test results and judge progress by that.

**Which AI platforms should I track?**

Start with the tools your buyers use most. For many businesses that means ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Perplexity. Add Claude and Copilot if your audience uses them. Ask the same set of questions on each platform every month so the results are comparable.
